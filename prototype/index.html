Direction: Wall Calendar

Monetra borrows from the giveaway wall calendar found in Indonesian homes: a saturated band at the top, heavy condensed numerals, and red dates. Here a red date is a day spent above the daily pace, so the month reads at a glance.

Red is reserved for overspending and negative balances. Expenses are ink with a minus sign, income is green with a plus sign, and transfers stay grey because they are neither. Every amount is set in tabular figures on a shared right edge.
